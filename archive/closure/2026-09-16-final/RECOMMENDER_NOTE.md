# Note for a recommender

The student closed a first-principles Monte Carlo acceleration attempt
without inflating results. They found real native bugs, implemented an
exact-corrected delayed-acceptance method, produced LiF-electron
formation energies at a frozen discrete Hamiltonian, and **refused** to
call a ~4% wall-time point estimate a success when the predeclared
interval and HAC diagnostics did not pass. That combination—technical
depth plus statistical honesty—is the letter-worthy part, not a claim
that FEP-DMC was generally accelerated.
